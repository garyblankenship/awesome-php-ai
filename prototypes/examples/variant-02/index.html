The province of PHP, newly animated.

For two decades PHP powered the web's form handlers and template engines. Now it is quietly becoming a creditable surface for language models — agentic frameworks, provider clients, and retrieval stacks have arrived, and they are written in the language an enormous share of the internet already speaks.

This issue surveys the field: full frameworks that wrap a dozen providers behind one interface; thin clients that speak fluent OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and Ollama; and the Model Context Protocol tooling that lets PHP participate in the agent era. What follows is a bounded reading list — not exhaustive, but representative of where the work is sharpest.
